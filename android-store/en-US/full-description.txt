FastPilot brings every race session together in one place: positions, telemetry, tire strategy, team radio, weather and championship standings. Follow the session live or go back to any race, practice or qualifying from past seasons.

DASHBOARD
• Running order with the interval and the gap to the leader
• Session events as they happen: pit stops, overtakes and race control messages
• Live session indicator

TELEMETRY
• Speed, throttle, brake and gear charts, lap by lap
• Theoretical best lap: the sum of the best sectors compared with the best actual lap

TIRE STRATEGY
• Each driver's stints, with the compound and the laps of use
• Degradation calculated from the clean laps of each stint
• Clean pace: no opening lap, pit entries and exits, safety car or red flag

DRIVER DUEL
• Compare two drivers side by side
• Cumulative delta across the race, average pace, best lap and valid laps

TEAM RADIO
• Listen to the messages between drivers and engineers right in the app
• Filter by driver

WEATHER
• Air and track temperature, humidity, rain, wind and pressure
• History of the readings throughout the session

STANDINGS
• Drivers' and constructors' tables, season by season

BUILT FOR RACE DAY
• Works offline: sessions you have already opened are saved on your device
• Available in English, Portuguese and Spanish
• Sign in with your Google account or use it as a guest, no sign-up needed
• No ads and no tracking
